# Glaral — User Acquisition Plan

*Written 2026-09-30. Target: worldwide Hindu diaspora (US, UK, Canada, UAE, Singapore, Australia first).*

## Positioning (from competitor research)

Every rival is free-with-ads or subscription. Nobody offers one-time. Glaral's line:

> **"The panchang app with zero ads. Pay once ($6.99), own forever."**

Free tier stays ad-free forever — it's the #1 complaint about every competitor and our sharpest marketing weapon.

## What's already in the product (v2)

- **ICS export** — "Add all festivals to my calendar" downloads a `.ics` for the year. This is MyPanchang's killer feature; it puts Glaral inside the user's Google/Apple Calendar = free daily impressions.
- **Live muhurta countdowns** — "Rahu Kaal ends in 42 min" on the Today view (stolen from Drik, the gold standard).
- **Festival countdown + upcoming strip** on the free Today tab — the teaser that sells Pro.
- **424 cities** — all Indian state capitals + tier-2/3 + full diaspora coverage (US/UK/CA/UAE/SG/MY/FJ/MU/TT/GY/NL/AU/NZ/ZA/NE).

## Channel plan (cheapest first)

### 1. Temple & community partnerships (free, highest intent)
- US/UK/CA temples publish festival dates — offer them a **free embeddable Glaral festival widget** (iframe) for their website in exchange for a link. Temples = trusted distribution to exactly the right audience.
- MyPanchang proves temples already link to diaspora-first date sources.

### 2. SEO — festival date pages (free, compounds)
- One page per major festival per year: "Diwali 2026 date, muhurat, city-wise timings". 122 festivals × years = programmatic SEO moat.
- Target long-tails competitors ignore: "when is [festival] in [city]" (e.g., "Diwali 2026 date in New Jersey").

### 3. Reddit & communities (free)
- r/hinduism, r/IndiaSpeaks, diaspora subs (r/ABCDesis, r/Brampton etc.): value-first posts — "I built an ad-free panchang, here's the ICS for 2026 festivals" (not ads, genuine utility).
- WhatsApp community forwards: the ICS file is inherently shareable.

### 4. Instagram/TikTok (free, Deep's home turf)
- 15-sec Reels: "Stop googling 'when is Diwali' — this tells you in 2 taps." Screen-record the countdown strip.
- Festival-eve posts ride the search spike (Diwali week = peak "diwali date" searches).

### 5. Product Hunt / BetaList launch (free)
- Launch as "the ad-free Hindu calendar, $6.99 once". The anti-subscription angle plays well with PH's audience.

### 6. Paid (only after organic proves conversion)
- Google Search ads on "[festival] 2026 date" during peak weeks (Oct–Nov). Tiny budgets, extreme intent.
- Meta ads to NRI interest audiences only if CAC < $2 (at $6.99, need 35%+ conversion — unlikely; test small).

## Pricing (validated)

**$6.99 one-time. Keep it.** Drik charges ~$2.40/yr just to remove ads — $6.99 ≈ 3 years of Drik, then free forever. No one-time competitor exists; $6.99 sits under the $9.99 impulse barrier ("less than a coffee" in the US). India: ~₹580 once vs ₹199/yr recurring.

## Roadmap (build order from research)

1. ✅ ICS subscribe — shipped in v2
2. ✅ Live muhurta countdowns — shipped in v2
3. **My Tithi** — save birthdays/anniversaries/shraddha by tithi with reminders. The retention moat (nobody does it well).
4. **Tara-bala strip** — "is today good for *me*?" via birth nakshatra. Cheap to compute, feels personal.
5. **Embeddable temple widget** — distribution via temples.
6. **Yearly personalized muhurat PDF** — AstroSage proves users pay ~₹1,000 for personalized reports; natural second product.

## Metrics to watch

- Free → Pro conversion rate (target: 3–5% of active users)
- ICS downloads (distribution proxy)
- Returning users on festival days (retention)
- Temple widget embeds (partnership traction)
